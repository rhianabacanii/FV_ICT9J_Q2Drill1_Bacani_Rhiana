First Hit: A Begginer's Guide to Volleyball




Things to knows

1. Each team is required to have six players to play
2. When serving, you have eight seconds until you put the ball over the net. If it goes outside the lines or under the net, the poit goes to the opposing team.
3. The ball can stay on one side of the court until three touches. On the third touch, the ball must go over the net.
☃️ Equipment

1. Ice skates that fit properly and provide ankle support
2. Warm clothing, including gloves, beanie/hat, leg warmers and layers to stay comfortable in cold temperatures.
3. A helmet, knee pads, and wrist guards for safety
4. A skating aid or support for beginners, such as a walker or a chair
5. A water bottle to stay hydrated during practice.
Strategies and Techiniques

1. When preparing to bump, bend your lges and put your feet apart.
2. put your left hand on top of your right hand and put your thumbs together to dig.
3. to set, place you hands on top of your forehead as if you're making something stop from goin to your eyes. There should be a triangle in the middle.
4. You can practice these steps with a friend or a wall to improve your skills
